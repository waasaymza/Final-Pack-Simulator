What this run is telling you

    How the line is modeled

    Each Mamata drops pouches onto a short belt into its Loma. Rejected pouches leave the line at the Loma. Good pouches build up on that lane's belt to the BPA. If a belt fills and its overflow tote is turned on, extra pouches go to the tote and the Mamata keeps running. With the tote turned off, the Loma backs up, then the Mamata stops and needs the restart time before it runs again.

    The gantry loads a cartridge with 7 or 9 slots. Each pick takes that many pouches from one lane's belt and stacks one pouch in every slot. It alternates lanes and keeps going on one lane if the other is down. After 7 or 8 picks (the customer's pouches per case) the stacks are pushed into the cases together. While the gantry loads the next cartridge, the case side pushes the full cases out to the taper and pulls a new set of empties in, so the gantry only waits if the case swap takes longer than loading the cartridge.

    The taper, printer + checkweigher, and palletizer each work one case at a time at their set speed. When a pallet is full, the palletizer pauses while the pallet is pushed out and the robot places a new pallet and slip sheet.
